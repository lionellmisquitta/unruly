## Engineering fields and scope consequences

Keep source behavior and our supported subset distinct. Before final DUX1 readiness, resolve the researched lock mismatch explicitly: Procreate protects editing, moving and deletion of locked layers; the earlier draft permits ordering/properties. Do not call that draft exact native lock parity. Layer deletion is a board command within our bounded undo, while document deletion is a separate persistence operation; design them independently.

For later formats, record document version, dimensions/export bounds, units/DPI, colour model/profile, transparent background, layer order/name/visibility/opacity/blend, editable versus flattened objects, and private/reference exclusion flags. Separate a portable editable package from flattened sharing outputs; parser acceptance is not a lossless import/export proof. Sanitized diagnostics and recovery exports remain explicit user actions. Schema and renderer changes require independent legacy pixel, quota/conflict/migration and cache-budget regression.

Keep four masking concepts separate: lock, alpha restriction, attached grayscale mask and independently ordered clipping layer. A future mask schema must encode relationships and reorder/deletion/transform effects, not overload visibility. Do not conflate reference-fill linework with a floating visual reference image. The official Options and Mask articles describe mask attachment positions inconsistently; use a deliberate tested relationship model instead of deriving a schema from one positional sentence.

Keep process recording separate from layer/frame animation and screen capture. Record/export exclusion is not encryption. Recording pause must not erase data; purge needs explicit irreversible confirmation. Future recording settings require a bounded storage/codec/preservation contract before renderer hooks. Full native3D/cameraAR was read and remains outside current product scope.

Current DUX1 remains compact layers/layout, original preset/colour controls and bounded gesture/shape/vector transforms. Other researched capabilities stay in their original roadmap or a separately reviewed later checkpoint. Physical Surface/Xiaomi and any browser-reserved shortcut/device hover behavior need actual device evidence. No code, QA executable, workflow, publication or contact action was performed for this research review. Production/main/G14/full checkpoint closure remain false.
