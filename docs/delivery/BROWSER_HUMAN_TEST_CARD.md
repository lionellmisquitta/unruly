# Three tasks after the verified URL is published

This card is for one batch of physical feedback, not per-checkpoint supervision. Nothing needs compiling or installing. Automated Chrome tests used synthetic input; Surface/Lenovo/Xiaomi hardware pressure and drawing feel remain NOT_VERIFIED.

1. On your Surface in Chrome, write a few sentences, make small loops and vary pen pressure. Lift/reposition the pen, then use fingers to pan/pinch. Tell us whether strokes disappear, connect across lifts, lag or respond unexpectedly to your palm, and whether reported pressure actually changes.
2. Use Undo, Redo and the whole-stroke eraser. Create another board, rename it, reopen the original, and reload the page. Export an important board as JSON. Wait for “Ready for offline use”, turn network off and reopen the same URL. Confirm the drawing remains. Browser data clearing can remove local boards; keep exports.
3. Open the URL on your Xiaomi tablet and repeat a short writing sample and two-finger navigation. Compare control reachability, pressure and feel against Surface. Drive sync is deferred, so the two browsers have separate local boards in this preview.

Return pointed feedback once for this batch. Advanced brushes, layers, imports, Drive and AI are still later features; no claim of Procreate/Clip Studio format compatibility or completed product checkpoint.
