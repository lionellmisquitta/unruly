## Outcome
Reusable movable skeleton references for drawing human/cartoon/anime characters and zoo animals. Extract permitted joint annotations or motion frames; do not require the source ML application, training, inference, subscriptions or runtime AI calls.

## Required behavior
- Separate Pose mode (move joints while preserving bone lengths) and Proportions mode (head/body ratio, torso, neck, shoulder/hip width and limb lengths). Save character profiles independently of poses; support adult, child, chibi and custom proportions.
- Retarget joint angles to profiles, with visible handling of changed hand/foot endpoints. Optional endpoint pins must report unreachable targets without stretching silently.
- Adjustable line of action, chest/shoulder and pelvis guides, hip shift and torso twist. Curved, angular, neutral and exaggerated styling are available to every character. Gender does not impose shoulder tilt, hip tilt or an S-curve.
- Move, scale, mirror, hide, lock and set opacity. For authored or imported 3D rigs, provide full 360-degree horizontal and vertical camera orbit, camera pan/zoom, perspective strength and orthographic view; freeze the selected projection as a drawing reference. Use a lightweight 3D skeleton with head/chest/pelvis forms underneath the canvas projection. Imported 2D-only annotations remain flat references and must not pretend to contain inferred depth. This owner-requested camera requirement supersedes the original 2D-only editor plan.
- Save edited poses and edited proportion sets independently under user-chosen names, with Save as new and explicit Update existing. Also save a combined reusable preset containing pose, proportions, attached prop and camera/view. Applying a different pose can retain the chosen character proportions; applying proportions can retain the chosen pose. Reopen/edit/resave and portable versioned library import/export are required.
- Skeletons are drawing references, with explicit exclusion from final artwork exports by default. Define persistence, backup, Undo and recording behavior before implementation; preserve original artwork and existing export behavior.
- Starter action categories explicitly include carrying, pulling, pushing, lifting, swinging an object, sword or hammer, crouching and exaggerated poses. Include one- and two-handed variants, standing/moving variants and anticipation/action/follow-through where relevant. Carrying references include a box/bag and a child; pushing/pulling include braced and leaning poses. Preserve guard, overhead/diagonal swing, thrust, lunge, airborne strike, landing, dodge, running and jumping. All actions are reusable across character proportions and gender presentations.
- Prop reference attachments support generic objects, swords and hammers with editable size/orientation and grip placement. Two-hand grip needs a specified pin/conflict policy. These are requested authored starter poses, not a claim that every external source contains them.
- Animal rig families: quadruped, primate, bird and reptile, with species profiles and optional appendage chains such as trunk, tail and wings. Do not force animals onto a human skeleton. Standing, walking, sitting, sleeping, eating, looking toward and interacting with a child remain required starter categories.

## Hair reference extension — owner request, 18 October 2026
POSE1A includes original parametric manga/chibi hairstyle guides: short spiky, pixie, bob, long flowing, curly clumps, ponytail, pigtails and bun, plus None. Adjust volume, length, parting and bangs. Hair follows head pose and proportion scale, supports camera orbit and close-up head study, and remains reference-only. Save hairstyle-only, character proportions+hair or full pose/profile/hair/camera; switching action poses preserves hairstyle. Legacy hairless presets remain importable. These are simplified silhouette/clump scaffolds for drawing, not finished character artwork or imported commercial assets. Clip Studio/VRoid compatibility is not claimed. External hairstyle assets remain evaluation-only until rights/format support established.

## Import contract and acceptance
Version, rig family/species, dimensionality (2D/3D), named view/camera parameters, normalized coordinate convention, semantic joint IDs, parent links, visibility/missing status, rest lengths, profile, tags and source URL/ID/license/attribution are required. Missing/occluded coordinates must not become real joints at zero. Motion conversion must preserve original provenance and distinguish sampled versus authored poses.

Reject malformed/nonfinite coordinates, cycles, duplicate joints, incompatible rigs and invalid lengths atomically. Verify fixed-length dragging, unreachable pins, left/right mirroring and mirror twice, profile round trips, pose retargeting, species-specific chains, independent and combined preset backup/reopen and offline use. Verify every requested action category has an authored starter; camera orbit/pan/zoom and perspective/orthographic projection preserve rig lengths, return to a saved view, and cannot be misapplied to 2D-only imports. Browser checks cover pen/phone selection, reference/artwork separation and default export exclusion. Preserve existing cumulative assertions and native recovery behavior.
