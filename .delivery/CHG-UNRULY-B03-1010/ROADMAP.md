# Remaining scope estimate — October 10

The cumulative drawing core is usable, not the full originally requested product. C04-B03 smudge is the current checkpoint, pending exact-source Chromium and hosted checks. Estimates are planning ranges, not approved fixed build counts or delivery dates.

After smudge: approximately 6–9 focused checkpoints for everyday personal drawing (layer masks, blur/adjustments, gap fill, text/reference content, common image/PDF exchange, tablet interaction/performance polish and practical backup/sync). The exact split depends on raster boundaries and Drive authorization.

Approximately 12–20 further checkpoints for the complete browser vision: those capabilities plus full whiteboard objects/connectors, conflict-preserving Drive sync, broader file exchange, motion-trace presentation, timelapse/video, BYOK AI and final regression/device acceptance. Native Windows/Android packaging and proprietary brush/file parity require separate feasibility and toolchain decisions and are not included in this browser estimate. Whole-canvas rotation remains deferred to polish.
